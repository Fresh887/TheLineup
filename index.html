The Lineup

Eleven riddles stand in a single line. Work out who stands where.

How it works

  • Each tile shows two pictures. Each half is a riddle, not a drawing: it may be a sound, a job, a clue or a joke about the thing. Put the two halves together and they join into one compound word. The blanks tell you how many letters it has.
  • Every word gives you its first letter. Type the word to reveal it.
  • Tiles are labelled with Greek letters. The clues use those labels and fix the order of the line. Together they allow only one line-up.
  • Read the letters from first place to last. The result is two words.

Clues

  1. γ and ι stand at opposite ends of the line.
  2. β stands immediately after κ.
  3. η stands directly between α and λ.
  4. ε stands immediately after θ.
  5. Two tiles end with the same word. They stand exactly seven places apart.
  6. δ stands exactly three places after α.
  7. θ stands somewhere after λ.
  8. γ stands somewhere before ζ.
